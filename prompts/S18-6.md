ISSUE 2 — DELAYED TARGET SAMPLING
==================================================

Add an OPTIONAL lifecycle argument:

--min-abs-initial-delay-seconds N

Default:

0

Meaning:

When selecting a lifecycle target, require the selected ARRIVAL or DEPARTURE
event to have an absolute GTFS-RT delay of at least N seconds.

For ARRIVAL:
use arrival.delay.

For DEPARTURE:
use departure.delay.

Do not infer delay by subtracting timestamps when the source delay field is
available.

If the required delay threshold is not met by any otherwise suitable target,
return:

Do not change existing behavior when the option is omitted.
