## 9. Attempt/retry reconciliation

For every target audit, reconcile:

- target execution
- HTTP/transport attempts
- attempt numbers
- response/failure categories
- retry decision

- quota permit evidence where available

- retry after 401/403;
- duplicate target transport for one target/bucket.
