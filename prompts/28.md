==================================================
FORMAL SCOPE DEFINITIONS
==================================================

Approve and document separate concepts.

1. COLOGNE STOP

A source stop whose actual stop_id matches:

de:05315:

2. COLOGNE SCHEDULED STOP EVENT

A stop_times event whose referenced stop_id is a validated Cologne stop.

3. COLOGNE INTERNAL SEGMENT

Two consecutive scheduled stop events of the same trip where BOTH endpoints
are Cologne stops.

This is the default technical rule for an internal movement/segment claim.

4. COLOGNE-SERVING TRIP

A trip having at least one Cologne scheduled stop event.

This is a technical discovery/identity label.

It does NOT mean the entire trip is analytically inside Cologne.

5. COLOGNE-INTERNAL TRIP

All source stop events of the trip are inside Cologne.

6. CROSS-BOUNDARY COLOGNE TRIP

The trip has both Cologne and non-Cologne stop events.

Only its validated Cologne events and Cologne-internal segments belong to core
Cologne KPI scope.

7. COLOGNE TOUCH-ONLY TRIP

A trip has a Cologne stop event but no Cologne-internal segment.

The stop-event may be valid Cologne evidence.

The whole trip or an external movement must NOT become an internal Cologne
movement.

8. COLOGNE-SERVING ROUTE

At least one trip on the source route has a Cologne stop event.

This is a technical route-discovery label only.

It MUST NOT mean:

"the complete physical route is inside Cologne."
