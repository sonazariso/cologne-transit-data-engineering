WHY 02A.3 IS REQUIRED
==================================================

The successful controlled current response established:

- TimetabledTime: observed
- EstimatedTime: observed
- RecordedAtTime: not observed in that response
- direct delay field: not observed

TRIAS 1.2 defines:

TimetabledTime = scheduled/timetable time
EstimatedTime = expected/predicted time
RecordedAtTime = actual/recorded time

RecordedAtTime is optional.

The absence of RecordedAtTime from one current StopEvent response does NOT
prove that:

- MDD never provides actual timing
- operators do not record actual arrival/departure times
- historical events lack RecordedAtTime
- another temporal request cannot expose it

TRIAS 1.2 StopEvent supports a requested LocationContext time.

The specification also defines relevant StopEvent errors including:

STOPEVENT_DATEOUTOFRANGE
STOPEVENT_NOEVENTFOUND
STOPEVENT_NOREALTIME

These allow historical availability to be tested rather than assumed.

A. RecordedAtTime available historically

  and quota impact before relying on retrospective retrieval.

B. Historical realtime exists but RecordedAtTime does not

Implication:
- archived estimates must remain estimates.
- they cannot become Actual Delay.
- collector design must preserve predictions if prediction history/accuracy is
  desired.
- another source would still be needed for actual performance.

C. Only historical timetable exists

Implication:
- retrospective MDD queries do not reconstruct realtime operational history.
- realtime collector becomes important for retaining operational evidence from
  collection time onward.

D. History is unavailable beyond a short window

Implication:
- collector retention becomes critical because MDD cannot be assumed to serve
  long-term historical operational data.

E. Historical request is ignored/falls back to current data

Implication:
- StopEvent cannot be treated as a historical archive based on HTTP success
  alone.

These are conditional implications only.

Do not choose one until the host probe has run and the raw responses have been
audited.
